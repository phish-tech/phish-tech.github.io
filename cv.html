Research experience

Physical Feasibility Evaluation of Medical LLMs and Physiological Feedback Alignment

Project Leader · January 2026–present
Supervisor: Xuming Hu, HKUST (Guangzhou)

  • Developed PhysDox to evaluate physical feasibility auditing of physiological sensing protocols across six modalities. Accepted to Findings of EMNLP 2026; co-first and corresponding author.
  • Investigating uncertainty-aware physiological-state representations from webcam-based iPPG, including signal quality, cross-subject variability, and confidence-calibrated feedback.

A Dual-modality Approach for Non-contact Blood Pressure Measurement

Core Member (2024) / Project Leader (2025) · September 2023–June 2025
Supervisor: Xiaorong Ding, UESTC

  • Developed a contactless blood pressure monitoring framework integrating camera-based iPPG and mmWave radar, spanning synchronized acquisition, waveform extraction, multimodal fusion, and evaluation.
  • Work resulted in publications in IEEE Sensors Journal and IEEE EMBC.

An Improved VMD Algorithm for mmWave Based Heartbeat Measurement

Project Leader · September 2024–June 2025
Supervisor: Shisheng Guo, UESTC

  • Developed an NRBO-optimized variational mode decomposition framework for heartbeat extraction from mmWave radar signals.
  • First-author oral paper at IEEE SMC 2025.
