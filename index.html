Boyuan Gu 顾博元

M.Phil. student in Data Science and Information Technology

I study how AI systems sense, interpret, and reason about human physiology.

I am a master’s student at Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School, Tsinghua University. I received my bachelor’s degree in Electronic Information Engineering through the joint education program between UESTC and the University of Glasgow, graduating with First-Class Honours.

My work connects contactless physiological sensing with reliable AI: using mmWave radar and iPPG to study cardiovascular monitoring, and evaluating whether language models understand the physical constraints of physiological sensing.
