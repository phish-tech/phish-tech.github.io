Understanding physiological signals.
Building reliable AI for healthcare.

I am a master’s student at Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School, Tsinghua University. I received my bachelor’s degree in Electronic Information Engineering through the joint education program between UESTC and the University of Glasgow, graduating with First-Class Honours.

My research brings together physiological sensing, signal processing, and machine learning. I work on contactless cardiovascular monitoring with mmWave radar and imaging photoplethysmography (iPPG), and on evaluating the physical feasibility of LLM-generated sensing protocols. My current interests include uncertainty modeling, cross-subject adaptation, and reliable physiological feedback for medical AI.
